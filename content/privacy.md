This is the personal academic website of Theodoros Karapanagiotidis. It does not use accounts, forms or advertising.

## Analytics

With your permission, the site uses Google Analytics to understand how it is used, for example which pages are visited and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as `_ga`) and receives information about your visit, including pages viewed, browser and device type, the referring site and an approximate location. IP addresses are anonymised.

These cookies are only set if you choose **Accept** in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page; declining removes the analytics cookies.

## Preferences stored in your browser

Your theme choice (light or dark) and your cookie choice are saved in your browser's local storage. They are never sent anywhere.

## Content from other services

Some pages load content directly from other services, which then receive your IP address as part of the request:

- Publication pages show citation badges from Altmetric and Dimensions.
- The contact section shows a map from OpenStreetMap.

The site is hosted by Netlify, which processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.
